AquaClean Series 250 bridge — install

Experimental firmware for the Geberit AquaClean Alba (Series 250). Flashing unofficial firmware may affect your device's warranty and your installer's service terms. Use at your own risk.

Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer (not a phone, not Safari/Firefox). Plug the ESP32-S3 board into a USB port first.

1. Flash

Click the button, pick the board's serial port when asked, and let it install.

2. Set up & watch the log (no cable)

  1. On your phone or laptop, join the Wi-Fi network AquaClean-Setup (password aquaclean250).
  2. Open http://192.168.4.1/ in any browser.
  3. Enter your toilet's BLE address and press Save (the board reboots).
    How do I find my toilet's BLE address?

    It's a six-pair code like e4:85:01:cd:c4:1e. Install a free Bluetooth scanner app (nRF Connect by Nordic Semiconductor, on iOS and Android). Stand next to the toilet, move the remote a few metres away, and tap Scan. Your toilet is the strongest nearby device, usually with an address starting e4:85:01 (Geberit). If two share that prefix, one is the remote — the toilet is the one that stays strongest by the toilet, and the one the Geberit app connects to.

  4. Re-join the network, reopen the page, and follow the test plan.
